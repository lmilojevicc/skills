# Brand Name Creation

Creation only. No availability, trademark, domain, or language-negative checks - treated as common sense, handled elsewhere.

Two sources, both read in full:
- **Bell** gives the type system + what the name should say.
- **Placek (Lexicon, 4,000 names: Sonos, Azure, Vercel, Windsurf, Pentium, BlackBerry, PowerBook, Swiffer)** gives the invention process + sound engine + selection mindset.

## 1. Bell: pick a type before brainstorming

Seven categories, verbatim from transcript. Pick 1-2 deliberately.

1. **Eponymous** - Disney, Burberry, Adidas (Adi Dassler), Tesla (homage, died 1943). Embodies founder vision. "Okay if you're feeling lazy or just have a big ego."
2. **Descriptive** - American Airlines, Home Depot. Tells exactly what you do. Mouthful, hard to own.
3. **Acronym** - GE, BP, KFC (fried didn't sound healthy), HSBC (expand globally). Shorthand descriptive, sometimes strategic.
4. **Real word** - Uber (outstanding example, ambitions beyond ride-hailing), Slack. Ripped from dictionary, suggests attribute/benefit. Almost none left with 300M companies.
5. **Composite / Invented** - Facebook, Ray-Ban (glue two words, one-two punch, memorable); Kleenex, Pinterest (change/add/remove letters for impact). Highly unique. Risk: pharma or IKEA sofa.
6. **Associative incl. foreign** - Amazon (largest river -> biggest selection), Sirius (brightest star -> brightest stars of music), Red Bull (power/confidence). Foreign: Samsung (three stars, Korean), Lego (play well, Danish), Zappos (zapato/shoes, Spanish), Hulu (precious bowl, Chinese proverb). Reflects imagery back to brand.
7. **Abstract** - Rolex, Kodak. No intrinsic meaning, relies on power of phonetics.

Default for crowded markets: 5/6/7.

## 2. Bell: big idea, not description

> "Tempting to talk about who created them or what you do or where you operate. But the best brand names don't describe. They stand for a big idea, one that translates into emotional appeal."

- Nike = winning
- GoPro = heroism
- Apple = simplicity and usability
- Google = googol (1 with 100 zeros) = organize the world's information

Ask: "What's your big idea?" Generate from that, never from a feature list.

Creation audit (from Alphabet case, domain point removed):
- Is it an idea? (alphabet = basis of language/communication)
- Playful link back to the family? (G for Google, C for Calico, N for Nest, X)
- Does it flatter the buyer? (alpha bet = outperform)

## 3. Placek: identify before invent

Process is identify -> invent -> implement. Creation uses the first two.

- Start with behavior, not positioning/values/mission (old thinking, worked 30 years ago, won't create Sonos today). Ask: how do you behave now / want to behave in future? How should the marketplace behave toward you? What experience are you creating? Name = experience with unlimited value, not a word with limited value.
- Map competition: brand names + language in the space. If it isn't distinctive you lose; imitation is suicide. Output is a **creative framework** (a window, not objectives) with depth/breadth/personalities. Get sign-off, then invent.

DIY version - the diamond (draw it, 4-5 days, not one hour):
- Top: WIN - how do we define winning?
- Right: what do we HAVE to win? (what makes us a winner already)
- Bottom: what do we NEED to win? (correct: not a good name, the right name)
- Left: what do we HAVE TO SAY to win? Spend most time here. Probe with metaphors until it becomes behavior/experience.

## 4. Placek: invent with small misdirected teams + volume

- No large brainstorms. 2-3 teams of 2. Team 1 gets the real brief. Team 2 gets a disguised brief (naming for Apple while working for Microsoft). Team 3 leaves the category entirely (name a bicycle/car/cup). Most winners come from team 2/3 - freedom to make mistakes opens creativity.
- Rule for intangibles: if you can't hold it (code process, cloud), make it tangible. Windsurf ex-Codium: brief was flow - smoother/dynamic coding. One team listed everything communicating flow: real word (flow), metaphor, sport/movement. Windsurf was sitting on that list.
- People: curious, tenacious, low-ego, fast writers (reporters, novelists, speechwriters). Must churn 200-300 names in 3-4 days and keep going after rejection.
- Volume: 3,000-4,000 ideas/directions at top of funnel (not shippable names), ~250 potential diamonds to fracture/examine. 200 is not enough - get to 1,000-1,500. Don't evaluate, speculate: what could we do with this? Build prototypes early (cap, t-shirt, ad mockup, wave imagery for Windsurf) - if you can't execute it, it's not the name.
- Force synchronicity: naming sailboats? Read hunting + flying magazines, notebook out, collect liked words. Unrelated dots connect to something you never would have thought of.

## 5. Placek: sound symbolism + compounding (the linguistic engine)

18,000 morphemes, 250+ linguists over 40 years. Each letter sends a signal/vibration. Set letter priorities before generating.

- **V** = most alive/vibrant in English (any country). Corvette (fast, roaring engine), Viagra, Vercel.
- **B** = most reliable. BlackBerry (double-B + black tech + compound leaned people in).
- **Z / S / V** = noisy = strong signal in busy markets. Azure sold on this, not meaning.
- **X** = fast, crisp + innovation (aircraft to computers).
- Balance noisy + smooth flow. Azure: A start + Z noise + smooth -ure ending.
- **Processing fluency**: lazy brain leans into easy-to-process, walks past complex. Complicated = liability. Vercel: familiar `ver` (truth, verde/green, over-, ever-) + `cel` (accelerate = what they do). Coined, but simple parts snapped together. Permission to create new + fluency = easy grasp.

Compounds are multipliers (research-backed): PowerBook, Facebook, DreamWorks, Windsurf. Wind + surf imagery circles stack: 1+1=3. Clients object "too long, want single word" - answer with multiplier + execution (imagery is trivial to execute). Sonos bonus device: palindrome-like (flip/turn upside down, same) + sound + flow of entertainment (not an entertainment company - name the marketplace, not yourself).

Name recipes that fall out of both videos:
- Familiar root + energetic tail (ver + cel)
- Image + experience compound (wind + surf)
- Blue-sky link for permission + linguistic balance for the sell (az + ure)
- Sound-first with story (son + os)

## 6. Placek: selection mindset while creating

- You won't know it when you see it. Almost never happens across 4,000 projects. Comfort-seeking kills bold names (Harry Potter rejected 16-18x, London's dog-becomes-wolf rejected more).
- There is no power in comfort. If the team is comfortable, you don't have it yet.
- Look for polarization/tension - sign of strength/energy in the word. Pentium beat Prochip (premium+professional+chip) because Andy Grove saw the split and picked energy.
- Humans notice only what is new/different (red shoe among black shoes). Be different from the list AND from the marketplace.
- Competitor test, don't ask "what do you think of X?" Ask: "our new competitor is called X - what do you think?" You're measuring what it does for them, how it fires imagination.
- Winning consumer sentence: "I don't know much about it, but they're not like the other guys." That predisposition to consider is the job. Descriptive (Cloud Pro among 10 clouds, InfoSeek) can never do it - start a story, don't make a statement.

## Creation workflow for agent

1. Clarify: offer, who must trust it, 1-2 vibe words, length/sound constraints, words to avoid.
2. Pick type(s) from section 1. Define big idea in 3-5 words (section 2). Run diamond (section 3), frame as behavior/experience.
3. Set sound priorities (section 5): pick 2-3 target letters + balance rule + fluency check.
4. Generate in 3 misdirected directions (section 4): literal brief, adjacent-category brief, far-field (sport/nature/object) brief. 20-30 candidates, each tagged: type + big-idea link + sound logic + recipe used.
5. Speculate, don't evaluate: for each, one-line execution (image, story, ad). Cut what can't be executed or what merely describes. Keep what polarizes and what makes outsiders say "not like the others."
6. Deliver top 10 with pronunciation, breakdown (roots like ver+cel), why it fits audience/vibe, risks. Ask user to pick 3.

## Anti-patterns (creation only)

- Brainstorming before picking a type.
- Positioning/values/mission brief instead of behavior/experience.
- One big brainstorm instead of small misdirected teams.
- Describing (Cloud Pro, InfoSeek, Codium) instead of story/experience (Azure, Google, Windsurf).
- Evaluating 200 names instead of generating 1,000+ then speculating.
- Complex hard-to-process constructions.
- Comfort-picking; discarding what scares the room.
- Asking "do you like it?" instead of competitor-test / imagination-fire test.
